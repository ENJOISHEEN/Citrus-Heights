Game Plan — Lock InSame standard from open to close

Every customer

  • Pitch the survey on every customer. No miss because of a language barrier, a long wait, or a "quick question."
  • Come around the counter. Set the expectation: "You will get a text. We are aiming for a 9 or 10."
  • Fix issues before the customer leaves. Not after.

Every ESCO / second exit interview

  • Manager or lead joins the floor and does the exit pitch before the customer walks out.
  • Goal: a verbal 9–10 commitment on each one.

Door and floor

  • Greeter in place every minute — open to close. The 10:00–11:30 gap is the first thing we fix.
  • Long waits on slow days hurt scores: check in line customers, set the wait time, take appointments first.

Accountability

  • Missed pitch = same-day coaching, documented.
  • Each rep says out loud at close: how many pitched, how many exit interviews.
  • Daily recap tracks it. Nobody waits for Saturday to fix it.
